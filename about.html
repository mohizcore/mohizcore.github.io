Edited for attention

I'm HyPhex — a short-form video editor working out of Pakistan, cutting for creators and small brands who need their content to actually hold a scroll.

I started cutting travel and nature transition reels less than two years ago. One of the earliest edits — built around simple whip-pan transitions and tight pacing — crossed 26K+ views with no paid promotion behind it. That's when the editing side of things became the actual focus.

Since then the work has moved into color grading, custom LUT packs, and full brand concepts — including a short-form concept built for an Asian bistro and hibachi grill. Everything runs through DaVinci Resolve, from the first cut to the final grade.

I also run a Hinglish life-advice Reels series alongside client work, which keeps the scripting and pacing muscles sharp between projects.
